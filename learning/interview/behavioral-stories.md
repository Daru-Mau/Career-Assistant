# Behavioral Stories

Write five stories in STAR format. Keep each under two minutes spoken. Use real facts only.

## STAR template

- **Situation:** context, team, constraints.
- **Task:** what you owned.
- **Action:** what you did, specific and technical.
- **Result:** measurable outcome, what changed, what you learned.

## Question mapping

| Question | Story |
|---|---|
| Tell me about a difficult bug | The Rooms |
| Tell me about a disagreement | CodeKataBattle |
| Tell me about learning something fast | AI Document Assistant |
| Tell me about incomplete requirements | Car Companion |
| Tell me about ownership | The Rooms |
| Tell me about a failure | Pick a real one, state the lesson |
| Why this role? | Connect the capstone and the thesis to the company |

## Rules

- Do not invent metrics. Use what you can verify.
- Prefer one concrete detail over three generic sentences.
- End with what you learned or would do differently.
- Rehearse out loud, not silently.
