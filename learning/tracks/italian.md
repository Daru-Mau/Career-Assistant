# Italian Track

Budget: 3 hours per week
Current level: B2
Target: solid B2+ in 12 weeks, C1 in 12 to 18 months.

## Weekly routine

| Activity | Frequency | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Grammar and vocabulary | Daily | 15 min |
| Listening or reading | Daily | 10 min |
| Writing a technical summary | 2 per week | 20 min each |
| Tutor conversation | 1 per week | 45 min |
| Recorded speaking sample | 1 per month | 5 min |

Total is about 3 hours when the tutor session is included.

## Exam plan

1. Weeks 1-4: diagnostic and grammar review. Take a CILS or CELI B2 reading and listening paper.
2. Weeks 5-8: writing and speaking focus. One full B2 mock under time pressure.
3. Weeks 9-12: full B2 mock exam, tutor feedback, decide whether to book the exam.
4. After week 12: if B2 is solid, start C1 materials. Otherwise consolidate B2 for one more quarter.

Do not book an exam before a full mock scores comfortably above the pass threshold.

## Progress markers

- [ ] Can explain the thesis and the capstone for five minutes without switching to English
- [ ] Can handle a technical interview in Italian for 30 minutes
- [ ] Can write a one-page technical summary with fewer than five recurring errors
- [ ] Can follow a Italian podcast or radio segment at normal speed
- [ ] B2 mock exam completed
- [ ] Tutor confirms B2+ speaking level

## Speaking prompts

Use these with the tutor or in recordings.

1. Spiega la tua tesi come se parlassi a un team di prodotto.
2. Descrivi un bug difficile e come lo hai risolto.
3. Perché vuoi lavorare su firmware o full-stack in Italia?
4. Descrivi l'architettura del tuo capstone in tre minuti.
5. Racconta un conflitto tecnico in un team e come lo avete risolto.
6. Spiega la differenza tra un processo bare-metal e un RTOS.
7. Convinci un cliente a scegliere una soluzione semplice invece di una complessa.

## Writing prompts

1. Riassumi un articolo de Il Post in 150 parole.
2. Scrivi una email a un recruiter per chiedere feedback.
3. Scrivi il README del capstone in italiano.
4. Descrivi un'architettura software in 200 parole.
5. Scrivi una cover letter di 250 parole per una posizione a Milano.
