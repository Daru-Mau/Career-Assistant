# Retail Roles

## Priorities

Emphasize:

- multilingual customer communication
- product comprehension
- active selling skills
- reliability and punctuality
- adaptability to varying shifts
- detail-oriented approach
- teamwork
- analytical problem-solving

CV length:

One page.

## Transferable Evidence

From CV:

- "carry out active selling"
- "order products"
- "product comprehension"
- "guarantee customer satisfaction"
- "identify customer's needs"
- "flexibility, adaptability and managing multiple priorities"
- "analytical thinking and problem solving"
- "detail oriented"

## Avoid

Do not claim:

- sales targets or KPIs met without evidence
- inventory management systems experience
- supervisory or managerial experience
- merchandising or visual merchandising experience
- cash handling experience unless explicitly asked
