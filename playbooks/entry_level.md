# Entry-Level and General Part-Time Roles

Use this playbook for temporary, entry-level or part-time roles outside the candidate's software engineering path. It covers kitchen and back-of-house work, waiting and bar service, warehouse and stockroom support, event staff and cleaning. For front-of-house hospitality use `hospitality.md`, for shop floor and sales use `retail.md`.

## Priorities

Emphasize:

- availability and flexibility across shifts, including evenings and weekends
- reliability and punctuality
- multilingual communication (list actual languages and levels)
- teamwork and following instructions
- ability to follow procedures and safety rules
- willingness to learn on the job
- attention to detail
- staying calm under pressure and during busy periods

CV length:

One page.

## Role Variants

- **Kitchen / back-of-house**: kitchen assistant, chef assistant, preparation support, dishwashing, basic cleaning under supervision.
- **Front of house**: waiter, runner, barista. Cross-check with `hospitality.md`.
- **Warehouse and operations**: warehouse assistant, picking and packing, stockroom support.
- **Events and services**: event staff, steward, porter, cleaning.

## Preferred Framing

- Keep the profile to two or three lines: availability, location, languages, reliability.
- Put languages and soft skills above technical projects.
- Compress software projects to one short line or omit them. For kitchen and warehouse roles a long list of programming projects distracts from the actual ask.
- Use education as evidence of discipline and ability to follow a structured process, not as a technical selling point.
- For kitchen roles, do not pretend to have culinary training. Lead with availability, willingness to learn and comfort in fast-paced environments.
- For warehouse roles, do not claim licences or equipment training that are not in the CV.

## Transferable Evidence

From CV (verify before use):

- Languages: Spanish native, English C1 (TOEFL certified), Italian B2, German and French working knowledge.
- Declared soft skills: Reliability, Teamwork, Adaptability, Attention to Detail, Problem Solving.
- Conducted a paired user study following a structured protocol, interacting with participants.
- Worked in multidisciplinary teams on software and design projects.
- Freelance data curation: followed a process and checked data quality and consistency.

No restaurant, kitchen, bar, warehouse, cleaning or customer-service employment is present in the CV. Do not present study participants as paying customers, and do not turn academic teamwork into professional shift work.

## Avoid

Do not claim:

- professional kitchen, restaurant or hotel experience
- culinary training, recipe development, knife skills or cooking techniques
- HACCP or food safety certification
- barista training or bartending skills
- cash handling, POS systems or active selling experience
- warehouse licences, forklift operation or heavy machinery
- supervisory or shift-leader experience
- physical stamina or heavy-lifting capacity not demonstrated
- driving licence, which is not listed in the CV
